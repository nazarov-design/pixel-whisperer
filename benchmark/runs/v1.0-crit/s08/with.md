## Desk crit: Where does our water go? · beginner (inferred from the work, so tell me if that's wrong)

### What works
- **The bar chart** (marker A). It is sorted from largest to smallest, each bar is labelled directly, and the percentage sits at the bar's end. No legend is needed, and it reads in about three seconds.
- **The three big numbers** (marker B). 300 L, 70% and 1 in 3 are large and bold, with a small caption under each. They give a general audience a fast way in before the detail.
- **One blue palette.** The colour matches the topic, and the dark-blue text on the pale background stays readable for the main content.

### Fix first
**1. The layout stops at about 66% of the page** (marker 1) · *Principle: composition*
- **What I see:** everything is packed into the top 680px and the bottom 300px is blank. The bars also end at x≈440, so the right side of that block is empty while the stats row above spans the full width.
- **Why it matters:** the page looks unfinished, as if the content ran out. It also forces the chart, your main content, to stay small.
- **Try this:** let the chart grow to fill the width, with bars running to about x=600. Or give the three tips more space and make them a proper closing section. Either way, decide the page's height first and then divide it up.

**2. The story is hidden in the colour fade** (marker 2) · *Principle: hierarchy*
- **What I see:** the bars fade from dark to pale. "Leaks" at 12% is very light, and "Other" at 8% is almost invisible.
- **Why it matters:** your own stat says 1 in 3 homes has a leak, and the tips tell people to fix leaks. So leaks is the message, yet it is the faintest thing on the page. The fade only repeats what the bar length already shows.
- **Try this:** make all bars one calm mid-blue and give "Leaks" a single accent colour (orange or red). Then the stat, the bar and the tip all point at the same thing.

**3. The small text is too small and too faint** (marker 3) · *Principle: typography and contrast*
- **What I see:** the tip text is about 11–12px, and the footnote "Illustrative numbers…" is pale blue on pale blue. In the title, "GO?" sits alone on the second line.
- **Why it matters:** the tips are the call to action, but they are the hardest thing to read. A general audience will skip them.
- **Try this:**
  - Raise the tips to at least 16px and make the icons smaller than the text block.
  - Darken the footnote.
  - Shorten or resize the title so it breaks as "Where does / our water go?", or fits on one line.

Later: the stats row and the bar labels use different left edges (centred columns versus a left-aligned list). Pick one axis.
