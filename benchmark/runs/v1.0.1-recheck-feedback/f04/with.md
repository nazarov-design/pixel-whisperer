### Keep as is
- A: Schedule table. It's the client's favorite, so only the colors are touched (#5 and #1a).
- B: The single "Book your first class" CTA. Orange is kept as the only accent, and that helps #1 and #2.

### Conflicts
- "Premium" vs "welcoming": too much restraint can turn cold. Resolve it with warm neutrals and real faces (teachers) rather than more color.
- Adding Teachers makes the page longer, which works against "less busy". Resolve it by cutting the cards to 3 and adding whitespace, so the page gets longer but not denser.

### Ask the client (max 3)
