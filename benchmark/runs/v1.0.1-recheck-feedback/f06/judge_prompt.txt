## Why it reads as spam

1. **Too many competing colors.** Red prices, red search button, red badges, navy, teal and yellow buttons, and yellow badges all compete. Nothing is clearly primary.
2. **Three equal-weight buttons on every card.** Details, Call and Save in three different colors turn each card into a row of ads. Save is the weakest action and has the loudest fill.
3. **Red prices.** Red signals sale or warning. Using it on every price cheapens the number, which should be the calmest element.
4. **Stacked badges over blank gray placeholder photos.** HOT!, NEW, EXCLUSIVE and -10% sit on empty images. Without real photos, the badges are the only thing you see.
5. **Cluttered nav and a cramped filter list.** There are 8 items, with "Call us" lost in the middle. The filters are a flat list of 10 tiny checkboxes with no grouping.
6. **Emoji as icons** (bed, bath, car) look cheap. The listing details are small and weak.

## The badge conflict

The client wants cleaner and bigger HOT / -10% badges at once. Making loud badges bigger works against "cleaner" and against "trustworthy". I'd raise this directly instead of just complying. Here is how I'd satisfy both:

- **Make fewer badges stand out.** Allow one badge per card, with a priority order such as price drop, then Hot, then New. Retire "EXCLUSIVE" as a badge and move it to a text label.
- **Make the two they care about bigger.** Increase them to about 13–14px text with more padding, placed consistently at the top-left of the image. Use one accent color for urgency (HOT, -10%) and a neutral tone for informational labels (NEW, EXCLUSIVE).
- **Show the -10% as a price.** Put the old price struck through next to the new one. That is more credible than a bare "-10%", and it is what real buyers trust.
- **Check the "convert" claim.** Ask whether the sales team has data for it, or whether it's an impression. I'd suggest an A/B test in a later round.

## Proposed changes

- **Palette:** navy plus one accent, used only for the primary CTA and urgency badges. Prices in dark neutral text. Drop the teal and yellow.
- **Card actions:** one primary button ("Request viewing" or "Details"), a secondary text or ghost "Call", and Save as a heart icon on the image.
- **Cards:** real photography (the biggest trust lever), a larger price, specs in a clean icon row, and the agent's name or photo for a human touch.
- **Trust signals:** license or years in business, review rating, and a phone number in the header.
- **Navigation:** reduce to 4–5 items, with a clear Call or Contact button.
- **Filters:** group them (Price, Rooms, Features), use collapsible sections, and show a result count.
- **Spacing and type:** more whitespace, a consistent type scale, and fewer font weights.
