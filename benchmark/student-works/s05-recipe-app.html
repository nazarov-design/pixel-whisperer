Spicy Tomato Shakshuka With Feta And Fresh Herbs

⏱ 30 min🔥 Medium👥 2 servings★ 4.8

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, chili flakes
  • 80 g feta, fresh parsley

STEPS

  1. Sauté onion and pepper in olive oil for 6–8 minutes until soft and slightly golden around the edges.
  2. Add garlic and spices, cook 1 minute, then pour in the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes until thickened.
  3. Make 4 wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5–7 minutes. Top with feta and herbs.
