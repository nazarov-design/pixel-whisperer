# Exercises

Pick one, adapt it to the student's own work. Search by area and level: `grep -i -A6 "hierarchy · beginner" exercises.md`. Every exercise is time-boxed and has a "done when" check. Levels: beginner (B), intermediate (I), advanced (A); an exercise marked for a level also works one level up with a tighter time box.

## Hierarchy

### E01 · hierarchy · beginner · 20 min · Squint & rank
Print or screenshot your work. Write down, in order, the 5 most important pieces of information. Then blur the image (or squint) and write what you actually see first, second, third.
Done when: you have both lists side by side and a new version where the first 3 seen = the first 3 important.

### E02 · hierarchy · beginner · 30 min · Three sizes only
Rebuild the layout using exactly three text sizes and two weights. No color changes allowed.
Done when: the order of reading is clear in greyscale, and you can name what each size is for.

### E03 · hierarchy · beginner · 40 min · One loud thing
Make 3 versions of your piece, each with a different single element as the clear focal point (e.g. headline, image, date). Everything else gets quieter.
Done when: in each version a friend can point to the focal point in under 2 seconds; you pick the best one and say why.

### E04 · hierarchy · intermediate · 45 min · Hierarchy with one tool
Make 4 versions of the same content, each creating hierarchy with only one tool: size, weight, color, or space.
Done when: all 4 read in the right order; you write one line on which tool suits this piece best.

### E05 · hierarchy · intermediate · 30 min · Five-second test
Show your screen to 3 people for 5 seconds. Ask: "What is this, and what would you do next?"
Done when: you have 3 answers written down and a list of what they missed; fix the top miss.

### E06 · hierarchy · advanced · 60 min · Dense screen, clear read
Take a dense screen (dashboard, table, settings) and reduce the number of distinct emphasis levels to 4 without removing content.
Done when: a style audit shows ≤4 text styles + 1 accent, and the primary task is the most prominent.

## Contrast

### E07 · contrast · beginner · 20 min · Contrast checker sweep
Check every text color against its background with a contrast checker. Log the ratio of each.
Done when: all body text is ≥4.5:1 and large text ≥3:1, with the before/after ratios listed.

### E08 · contrast · beginner · 30 min · Greyscale version
Convert your work to greyscale. Fix everything that disappears or merges.
Done when: the greyscale version reads in the same order as the color version.

### E09 · contrast · intermediate · 40 min · Make it obvious
Find every "polite difference" (e.g. 15 vs 16px, #333 vs #444, 8 vs 10px gaps). Either make each pair identical or clearly different (≥1.25× size, ≥2 weight steps).
Done when: a list of pairs, each resolved as "same" or "clearly different".

### E10 · contrast · intermediate · 30 min · Text over image
Place your headline over 3 different photos/patterns. Make it readable on all three using scrims, placement or crop, not by changing the text color only.
Done when: all three pass 4.5:1 at the text area.

### E11 · contrast · advanced · 45 min · Value study
Make a 5-value thumbnail (black, dark, mid, light, white) of your composition at 200px wide.
Done when: the thumbnail alone shows the focal point and main groups; adjust the full design to match it.

## Alignment

### E12 · alignment · beginner · 20 min · Count the edges
Draw vertical lines through every left edge in your layout. Count them.
Done when: you've reduced the count by at least half by snapping elements to shared edges.

### E13 · alignment · beginner · 30 min · No centering
Rebuild your layout with everything left-aligned. No centred text at all.
Done when: the layout feels ordered; you write one line on where (if anywhere) centring was actually better.

### E14 · alignment · beginner · 30 min · Invisible lines
Set up a 12-column grid with fixed margins. Re-place every element so its edges sit on column lines.
Done when: with the grid hidden, someone can trace at least 3 strong vertical lines.

### E15 · alignment · intermediate · 30 min · Table discipline
Take a table or list with numbers. Align text left, numbers right (tabular figures), headers to their column content.
Done when: decimals line up and every header shares an edge with its column.

### E16 · alignment · intermediate · 40 min · Card internals
Audit 3 cards/components: paddings, icon-to-text distance, baseline alignment of labels.
Done when: all cards share identical internal paddings and one spacing value per relationship.

### E17 · alignment · advanced · 45 min · Optical alignment
Find places where mathematically aligned elements look misaligned (round icons, quotes, capital A/V, play icons). Adjust optically.
Done when: a before/after with each optical correction annotated with its offset.

## Rhythm (spacing)

### E18 · rhythm · beginner · 20 min · Spacing scale
Replace every spacing value in your design with one from 8/16/24/32/48/64.
Done when: a list of all old values → new values, and no value outside the scale.

### E19 · rhythm · beginner · 30 min · Group by proximity
Without lines or boxes, separate groups of information only with space: small gaps inside groups, big gaps between.
Done when: someone can name the groups without any borders or backgrounds.

### E20 · rhythm · intermediate · 30 min · Remove the boxes
Remove every border, divider and card background. Rebuild grouping with spacing and alignment. Add back only what's truly needed.
Done when: at least half the boxes are gone and grouping is still clear.

### E21 · rhythm · intermediate · 40 min · Vertical rhythm
Set body line height on a 4 or 8px baseline grid. Align headings and paragraphs to it.
Done when: with the baseline grid visible, text sits on it consistently.

### E22 · rhythm · intermediate · 30 min · Section tempo
Look at the page as a sequence of sections. Vary their density deliberately (tight / spacious / full-bleed) instead of identical blocks.
Done when: a thumbnail strip of the sections shows a clear rhythm, not a uniform stack.

### E23 · rhythm · advanced · 60 min · Spacing tokens
Define spacing tokens (e.g. space-1…space-8) and their usage rules; apply them to the whole piece.
Done when: every spacing in the file maps to a token, with a one-page usage note.

## Typography

### E24 · typography · beginner · 20 min · Two fonts max
Reduce your work to one font family (two at most). Use weight and size for variety.
Done when: the work uses ≤2 families and still has clear levels.

### E25 · typography · beginner · 30 min · Readable body
Set your body text: 16–18px, line height 1.5, line length 45–75 characters.
Done when: you've measured a line (character count) and it's in range.

### E26 · typography · beginner · 30 min · Type scale
Pick a ratio (1.25 or 1.333) and generate a scale from 16px. Re-set all text using only those sizes.
Done when: every text size in the design is on the scale.

### E27 · typography · intermediate · 40 min · Specimen copy
Choose a typographic layout you admire (poster, editorial page). Recreate only its type: sizes, weights, spacing, with your own text. Don't copy imagery.
Done when: side by side, the type hierarchy matches; you list 3 things you learned.

### E28 · typography · intermediate · 30 min · Caps and tracking
Fix all uppercase text: add tracking (+5–10%), check line length, shorten labels.
Done when: no uppercase text longer than ~4 words, all tracked.

### E29 · typography · intermediate · 45 min · Pairing study
Make 3 versions of your headline + body with different pairings (sans/sans, serif/sans, mono accent). Keep layout identical.
Done when: you choose one and write why it fits the brief's mood.

### E30 · typography · advanced · 60 min · Details pass
Fix: widows/orphans, rags, hyphenation, quotes and dashes, number styles, optical sizes.
Done when: an annotated list of ≥10 fixes.

## Color

### E31 · color · beginner · 20 min · Palette diet
Reduce your palette to 1 neutral family + 1 accent. Every remaining color must have a named role.
Done when: a palette card with roles (background, text, accent, …) and the design uses only those.

### E32 · color · beginner · 30 min · Accent only for action
Use your accent color only for the primary action and one key highlight.
Done when: count of accent uses ≤3 on the screen.

### E33 · color · intermediate · 40 min · 60/30/10
Repaint your design with a 60/30/10 split (dominant / secondary / accent).
Done when: rough area estimate matches the split and the focal point is in the accent.

### E34 · color · intermediate · 30 min · Chart colors
Redo a chart with one hue for the main series and grey for the rest, plus direct labels instead of a legend.
Done when: the key insight is readable without a legend.

### E35 · color · intermediate · 40 min · Dark mode test
Make a dark version of one screen. Don't invert; rebuild with defined roles.
Done when: contrast passes and the hierarchy is the same as in light mode.

### E36 · color · advanced · 60 min · Semantic system
Define color tokens by role (surface, text, border, accent, success, warning, danger) with light/dark values.
Done when: every color in the design maps to a role token.

## Composition

### E37 · composition · beginner · 30 min · Thumbnails first
Sketch 6 thumbnail layouts (5×7 cm) of your piece in 15 minutes, before touching the software. Pick one and rebuild.
Done when: 6 thumbnails photographed + the rebuilt version.

### E38 · composition · beginner · 30 min · Edges and margins
Set equal, generous outer margins (at least 6% of the width). Nothing touches the edge unless it's a deliberate full bleed.
Done when: margins are measured and consistent on all sides.

### E39 · composition · intermediate · 40 min · Asymmetry study
Make an asymmetric version of your centered layout with a clear visual balance (big element vs group of small ones).
Done when: you can explain what balances what.

### E40 · composition · intermediate · 45 min · Crop studies
Take your main image and make 5 crops (tight, wide, off-centre, partial, detail). Place each in the layout.
Done when: you pick the crop that best supports the message and say why.

### E41 · composition · advanced · 60 min · Grid swap
Lay out the same content on 3 grids: 12-column, modular, and a single-column editorial grid.
Done when: 3 versions + a note on what each grid makes easy and hard.

### E42 · composition · advanced · 45 min · Remove 30%
Remove 30% of the elements without losing meaning. Use the space you gain.
Done when: list of removed items and a version that still communicates everything essential.

## Process & self-critique

### E43 · process · beginner · 20 min · Self-crit checklist
Before sharing your next work, write 3 sentences: what it's for, who it's for, what the viewer should do. Then check the design against them.
Done when: the sentences are written and you've changed at least one thing because of them.

### E44 · process · intermediate · 30 min · Reference board
Collect 8 references for one specific quality you're working on (e.g. "strong typographic hierarchy in posters"). Annotate each with one observation.
Done when: 8 references with one-line notes, none of them copied into the work.

### E45 · process · advanced · 45 min · Design rationale
Write a 200-word rationale for your work: decisions, trade-offs, what you'd do with more time.
Done when: each major visual decision is linked to the brief or the user.
