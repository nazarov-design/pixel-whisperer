### Fix first
**1. The venue is buried** (marker 1) · *Principle: hierarchy and contrast*
- **What I see:** the address and the rain plan are about 12px, in lilac on dark purple, in the last corner of the poster. The "Get tickets" button is also small and sits far from the date it belongs to.
- **Why it matters:** "where" is half of what someone needs from an event poster. It currently reads like legal small print.
- **Try this:** pull the venue up into a mid-size line under the date, in white or yellow. Keep the rain plan as the only small text. Move the button next to the date and venue.

**2. The gaps between groups are uneven** (marker 2) · *Principle: rhythm*
- **What I see:** "Summer Sessions" is jammed against ROOF. Then there's a ~150px hole before the date. The lineup then floats with another hole above the footer.
- **Why it matters:** equal-looking gaps make everything seem unrelated. Spacing should show which things belong together.
- **Try this:** make three groups:
  - title and subtitle
  - date, lineup and venue
  - tickets and small print

  Use a small gap inside each group and one big, repeated gap between groups.

**3. The circle has no job** (marker 3) · *Principle: composition*
- **What I see:** a big pink disc on the right, cropped by the edge, that relates to nothing in the text. It's the loudest color on the page and it competes with the headline. With the sax gone, nothing on the poster says "jazz" except the words.
- **Why it matters:** decoration should either carry the concept or support the hierarchy.
- **Try this:** make it a sun or a vinyl record, or tie it to the info, for example by putting the date on it. Otherwise shrink it and push it back.

Later: the left edges drift by a few pixels between blocks. Snap them to one line once the spacing is fixed.
