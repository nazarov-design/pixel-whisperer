Ember & Oak started as a cart at the Saturday market. Ten years later we still buy green coffee directly from three farms we have visited, roast it ourselves in the back room, and change the filter offer every two weeks. Our bakers start at five so the cardamom buns are still warm when the first commuters arrive. We are a small team of nine, and most of us have been here for more than five years. If you want to learn to brew at home, ask at the counter about our monthly Saturday workshops, where we cover grind size, ratios and water.
